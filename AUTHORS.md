# Authors and Contributions

This repository contains scripts developed by the **Bioinformatics Research Group at the Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES)** as part of the *DArTseq_InDel_Finder* project. It is the InDel/HRM evolution of the *DArTseq_SNP_Finder* project, and shares the same team.
